Our Story

Built on Trust & Innovation

FLOW was founded on a simple belief: technology should move your business forward.

The company was born from the partnership between Bokang, a business strategist with a deep understanding of client needs, and Theo Anold Gaopalelwe, a technical expert passionate about building robust digital solutions. Together, they created FLOW to bridge the gap between business ambitions and technical execution.

Starting in Botswana, FLOW's vision is to expand globally, providing professional websites, applications, and business systems that empower companies to compete in the digital age.
