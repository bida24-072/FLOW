How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last Updated: January 2024

1. Information We Collect

FLOW collects information to provide better services to our clients. We collect:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and company name when you contact us.
  • Project Information: Details about your project requirements and business needs.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our website (via cookies).

2. How We Use Your Information
