# FLOW Website
**Future • Logic • Operations • Worldwide**

## Overview
This is the official website for FLOW, a technology solutions company providing websites, applications, and business systems to businesses in Botswana and beyond.

## Pages
- `index.html` - Home Page
- `about.html` - About Us (Founders info)
- `services.html` - Services we offer
- `portfolio.html` - Demo projects
- `contact.html` - Contact form & WhatsApp

## Setup Instructions

### 1. Update Placeholders
Search for `[UPDATE THIS]` in all files and replace with your actual business information:
- Company email
- Phone number
- WhatsApp number
- Location
- Founder names and bios (in about.html)

### 2. Images
Replace Unsplash placeholder images with your own:
- Office team photo (Home page hero)
- Founders photos (About page)
- Demo project screenshots (Portfolio page)

### 3. Hosting
Upload all files to your web host. Recommended:
- Netlify (free)
- Vercel (free)
- Any cPanel hosting
